## Summary

Reserve Your Spot helps people discover local businesses and book appointments.
This policy explains what the app collects and why.

The current public build runs on **sample data only** and does not collect or
transmit any personal information. The sections below describe what the app
collects once connected to its live backend.

## Information we collect

When you create an account and use the live service, we collect:

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Name, email address, phone number | To create your account and let businesses identify your booking |
| Bookings you make (business, service, staff, date/time) | To provide the booking service and your history |
| Businesses you save as favourites | To show them on your Saved tab |
| Payment confirmation details | Deposits are processed by **Stripe**. We store only a payment reference and amount — never full card numbers |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use it

- To operate the booking service (create, show, cancel bookings).
- To let a business you book with see your name and booking details.
- To send booking confirmations and reminders.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** — database, authentication and file storage (data hosting).
- **Stripe** — payment processing for deposits. See stripe.com/privacy.

## Data retention & deletion

Your account data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account
and associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it
within 30 days.

## Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect their data.
